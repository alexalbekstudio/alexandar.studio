# 🔧 ServisMasterOS — User Guide

**Complete operating system for modern auto repair shop management.**

## 1. Introduction
ServisMasterOS is designed to replace paper work orders, Excel spreadsheets, and manual invoicing. The system manages vehicles, parts, mechanics, and finances.

## 2. First Steps (License Activation)
1. Open the application.
2. Log in with the default credentials (e.g., `admin@servis.rs` / `admin123`).
3. **IMPORTANT:** Immediately go to "Settings" and enter the **license key** you received upon purchase. Then, change your password and enter your company details (name, Tax ID, address, logo).

## 3. Vehicle Intake & Work Orders
1. Click the **"New Intake"** button.
2. Enter the license plate (or use the OCR scanner if you have Pro or Enterprise).
3. Take photos of any existing vehicle damage.
4. Select a mechanic and describe the fault.
5. Click **"Create Work Order"**.

## 4. Managing Work Orders
Update the status as you go: *Intake → Diagnostics → Waiting for Parts → In Progress → Ready*.
When you add a part to an order, the system automatically deducts it from your inventory.

## 5. Parts Inventory
- Each part has a SKU, name, purchase price, and selling price.
- The system alerts you when stock falls below the minimum threshold.
- Barcode scanning is supported.

## 6. Invoicing
At the end of a repair, generate a PDF estimate or invoice directly from the work order.

## 7. Analytics
Track daily, weekly, and monthly revenue, as well as mechanic productivity and most-used parts.
